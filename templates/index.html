URL pattern analysis

Think Before You Click.

Paste a link and LinkShield checks its text for patterns often seen in phishing, such as disguised IP addresses, look-alike domains and hidden redirects, then explains each finding in plain language. It never opens the link.

How It Works

  1. Validate. The URL is checked in the browser and again on the server.
  2. Apply rules. Each rule below that matches adds its points to the score.
  3. Explain. You get the score, a risk level and a plain-language reason for each finding.

Score bands: Low 0–{{ medium - 1 }} Medium {{ medium }}–{{ high - 1 }} High {{ high }}–100 The total is capped at 100. It is a heuristic, not a probability of an attack, and one weak signal is never enough for a high rating.
